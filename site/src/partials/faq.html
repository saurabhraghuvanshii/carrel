Questions

Short answers

Is it free?

Yes. Carrel is free and runs on your computer. There is no account to make.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Problems, tests and your code all stay on your computer. Only the optional AI hints need a network.

Which languages?

Java and C++ to start. Carrel uses the javac and g++ already installed on your computer. More languages are planned.

Where is my code?

In plain files in ~/.carrel/solutions. Open them anywhere, or put the folder in git.

Does it send my code anywhere?

No. If you turn on AI hints and press Ask, that one question goes to the provider you chose, using your own key.

Are these real company questions?

No. Every problem is written from scratch. Some follow patterns commonly seen in online assessments, but none are copied from any site or company.
