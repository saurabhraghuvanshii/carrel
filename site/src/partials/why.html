From the maker

Why I made this

I kept my practice in a folder of Java files. It worked for my code, and for nothing else. No problem text, no tests, no order to follow.

The places that had those wanted an account, then a subscription, then a good connection. I wanted something smaller. Open it, pick a problem, write code, see if it passes. On a train, on a weak connection, on a laptop that has nothing but a compiler.

So I built Carrel. It is one small program. It uses the compilers already on your computer, so there is nothing to sign in to and no sandbox to download. Your code is plain files in a folder you can open, copy, or put in git.

I made it calm on purpose. A warm page, one accent colour, and a tag on every problem so you know what you are walking into. Nothing flashes while you think.
