You stand on the first square of a row. The number on each square is the longest jump you can make from it; any shorter jump is allowed too. Return `true` if you can reach the last square, and `false` otherwise.

Walk left to right keeping the furthest square reachable so far. If you ever stand past that point, you are stuck.

## Constraints

- 1 ≤ length ≤ 10⁴
- 0 ≤ value ≤ 10
