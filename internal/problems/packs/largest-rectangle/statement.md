You are given the heights of bars in a bar chart, each one unit wide and standing side by side. Return the area of the largest rectangle that fits entirely inside the bars.

For each bar, the widest rectangle of its height reaches to the nearest lower bar on each side. A stack finds those for every bar in one pass.

## Constraints

- 1 ≤ number of bars ≤ 10⁴
- 0 ≤ height ≤ 10⁴
