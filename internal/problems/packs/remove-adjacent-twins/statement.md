You are given a word of lowercase letters. While the word has two equal letters next to each other, remove both of them. Return what is left once no such pair remains. The answer is shown in quotes, and it may be empty.

Removing one pair can bring two equal letters together, so think of the letters you have kept as a stack.

## Constraints

- 1 ≤ length ≤ 10⁴
- Only `a` to `z`
