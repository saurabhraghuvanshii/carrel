Robots stand in a row on a rail. Each is given as a nonzero whole number: its size is its strength, and its sign is its direction, positive for right and negative for left. All robots move at the same speed.

When a robot moving right meets one moving left, the weaker one is destroyed; if they are equally strong, both are. Robots moving the same way never meet. Return the robots that are left, in their order on the rail.

## Constraints

- 1 ≤ number of robots ≤ 10⁴
- 1 ≤ strength ≤ 1000
