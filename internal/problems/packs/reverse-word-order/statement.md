A text holds words made of letters and digits, separated by one or more spaces. There may also be spaces at the start or the end. Return the words in reverse order, separated by exactly one space, with no spaces at the ends.

Collect the words by skipping runs of spaces, then join them from last to first. For a follow-up, do it in place on a character array: reverse the whole text, then reverse each word back, and finally squeeze out the extra spaces.

## Constraints

- 1 ≤ text length ≤ 10⁴
- At least one word; only letters, digits and spaces
