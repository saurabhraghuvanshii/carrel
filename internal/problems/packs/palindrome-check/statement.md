You are given one line of text. Ignore every character that is not a letter or a digit, and treat upper and lower case letters as the same. Return `true` if what is left reads the same from the front and from the back, and `false` otherwise.

Text with no letters or digits at all counts as reading the same both ways.

## Constraints

- 1 ≤ length ≤ 2 × 10⁴
- Printable ASCII characters only
- The line does not start or end with a space

## Follow-up

Can you check it without building a cleaned copy of the text?
