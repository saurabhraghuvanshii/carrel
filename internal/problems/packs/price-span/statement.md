You are given the price of a product on each day. The span of a day is the number of days in a row, ending with that day, on which the price was at most that day's price. Return the span of every day.

Like "Days until warmer", but looking back. Equal prices count toward the span.

## Constraints

- 1 ≤ number of days ≤ 10⁴
- 1 ≤ price ≤ 10⁵
