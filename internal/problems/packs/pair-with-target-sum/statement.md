You are given a list of whole numbers and a target. Find the two positions whose values add up to the target and return them as a pair. Exactly one such pair exists, and you may not use the same position twice.

## Constraints

- 2 ≤ length ≤ 10⁴
- Values are between −10⁹ and 10⁹
- The target is between −10⁹ and 10⁹
- The pair may come back in any order
