A row of plants is described by a list of numbers, one per plant; equal numbers mean the same kind of plant. You may harvest one stretch of neighbouring plants, but your cart only takes two kinds. Return the length of the longest stretch that holds at most two different kinds.

## Constraints

- 1 ≤ number of plants ≤ 10⁴
- 0 ≤ kind ≤ 10⁴

## Follow-up

How would you change your answer to allow at most `k` kinds?
