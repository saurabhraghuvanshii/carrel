You are given encoded text made of lowercase letters, numbers and square brackets. `k[text]` means `text` repeated `k` times, and brackets can be nested. Return the decoded text.

Every number is followed by a bracket, every bracket holds at least one letter, and numbers have no leading zeros.

## Constraints

- 1 ≤ length of the encoded text ≤ 10⁴
- 1 ≤ k ≤ 30
- The decoded text has at most 2 × 10⁴ letters
