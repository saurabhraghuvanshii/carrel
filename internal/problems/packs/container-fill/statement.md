You are given the heights of walls standing in a row, one unit apart. Pick any two walls. With the ground, they make a tank: the water level is the height of the shorter wall, and the width is the distance between the two walls. Return the most water any such tank can hold, which is height × width.

## Constraints

- 2 ≤ number of walls ≤ 10⁴
- 0 ≤ height ≤ 10⁵

## Follow-up

Start with the two outer walls. Why is it always safe to move the shorter one inward?
