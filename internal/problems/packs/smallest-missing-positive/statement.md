You are given a list of whole numbers. Find the smallest positive whole number (1, 2, 3 and so on) that does not appear in the list.

Use time proportional to the length of the list and only a fixed amount of extra memory. You may change the list.

## Constraints

- 1 ≤ length ≤ 10⁴
- −10⁹ ≤ value ≤ 10⁹

## Follow-up

Why must the answer be between 1 and length + 1, and how does that let you use the list itself as a record of what you have seen?
