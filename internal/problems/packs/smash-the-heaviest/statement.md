You have a pile of stones with whole-number weights. Again and again, take the two heaviest stones and smash them together. If they weigh the same, both are destroyed. Otherwise the lighter one is destroyed and the heavier one loses that much weight. When at most one stone is left, return its weight, or 0 if none is left.

A max-heap gives you the two heaviest stones at every step.

## Constraints

- 1 ≤ number of stones ≤ 10⁴
- 1 ≤ weight ≤ 1000
