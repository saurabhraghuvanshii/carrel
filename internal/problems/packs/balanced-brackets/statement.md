You are given a word made only of the brackets `(`, `)`, `[`, `]`, `{` and `}`. Return `true` if every opening bracket is closed by the same kind of bracket, in the right order, and `false` otherwise.

Keep the brackets that are still open on a stack. A closing bracket must match the one on top.

## Constraints

- 1 ≤ length ≤ 10⁴
- Only the six bracket characters
