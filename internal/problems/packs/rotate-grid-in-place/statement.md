You are given a square grid of whole numbers. Turn it a quarter turn clockwise by changing the grid you were given, not by building a new one.

After the turn, the first row holds what was the first column, read from bottom to top.

## Constraints

- The grid is n by n, with 1 ≤ n ≤ 200
- −1000 ≤ value ≤ 1000

## Follow-up

How would you turn it a quarter turn the other way, or half a turn?
