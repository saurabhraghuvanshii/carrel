You are given a map as a grid of `1` (land) and `0` (water). An island is a group of land cells joined up, down, left or right; diagonal cells do not join. Return the number of islands.

When you find land you have not seen, count one island and flood-fill all of it, so its other cells are not counted again.

## Constraints

- 1 ≤ rows, cols ≤ 60
- Every cell is `0` or `1`
