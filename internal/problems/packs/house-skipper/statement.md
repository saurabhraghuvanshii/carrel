A row of stalls each holds some coins. You may collect from any stalls you like, but never from two stalls that stand side by side. Return the largest total you can collect.

For each stall there are two choices: take it, adding its coins to the best total that skipped the stall before; or skip it, keeping the best total so far. Two running numbers are enough.

## Constraints

- 1 ≤ length ≤ 10⁴
- 0 ≤ value ≤ 1000
