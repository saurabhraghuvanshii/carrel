You are given a list of whole numbers. Build a new list of the same length where each position holds the product of every value in the original list except the one at that position.

Do it without division, in time proportional to the length of the list.

## Constraints

- 2 ≤ length ≤ 10⁴
- −30 ≤ value ≤ 30
- Every product you need fits in a 32-bit signed integer

## Follow-up

Can you do it with no extra list besides the one you return?
