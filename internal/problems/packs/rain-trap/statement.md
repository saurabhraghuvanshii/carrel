You are given the heights of columns standing side by side, each one unit wide. When rain falls, water collects in the dips between taller columns and runs off at both ends. Return how many units of water stay.

The water above a column reaches the lower of the tallest column on its left and the tallest column on its right.

## Constraints

- 1 ≤ number of columns ≤ 10⁴
- 0 ≤ height ≤ 10⁵

## Follow-up

Like "Product of the others", you can answer with one pass from the left and one from the right, using two extra lists. Can two pointers do it with no extra list at all?
