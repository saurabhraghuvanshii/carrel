You are given a word of uppercase letters and a number `k`. You may change at most `k` letters, each to any uppercase letter. Return the length of the longest stretch of one repeated letter you can make.

A window can become one letter if its length minus the count of its most common letter is at most `k`.

## Constraints

- 1 ≤ length ≤ 10⁴
- 0 ≤ k ≤ length
- Only `A` to `Z`
