You are given a list of whole numbers, some of which may be negative, and a target. Count the stretches of one or more neighbouring values whose sum equals the target. Two stretches are different if they start or end at different positions.

## Constraints

- 1 ≤ length ≤ 10⁴
- −1000 ≤ value ≤ 1000
- −10⁷ ≤ target ≤ 10⁷

## Follow-up

A sliding window works when every value is positive. Why does it fail here?
