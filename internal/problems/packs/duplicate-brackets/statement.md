You are given a valid arithmetic expression made of single lowercase letters, the operators `+`, `-`, `*` and `/`, and round brackets. A pair of brackets is pointless if, apart from other bracket pairs, nothing sits directly inside it. For example, the outer pair in `((a+b))` only holds another pair.

Return `true` if the expression has at least one pointless pair, and `false` otherwise. Brackets around a single letter, as in `(a)`, are not pointless.

## Constraints

- 1 ≤ length ≤ 10⁴
- The brackets are balanced and the expression is valid
