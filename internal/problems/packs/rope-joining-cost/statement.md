You have ropes of different lengths and want to join them all into one. Joining two ropes costs the sum of their lengths, and the result is one rope of that length. Return the smallest total cost.

Every join adds its rope to all later costs, so short ropes should be joined first. Always join the two shortest ropes you have.

## Constraints

- 1 ≤ number of ropes ≤ 10⁴
- 1 ≤ length ≤ 1000
